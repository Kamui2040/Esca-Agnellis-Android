Esca Agnellis is a local Android app for friendly, low-pressure food-pyramid tracking.

The app works without an account and without Internet permission. Tracking data stays on the device. There are no ads, telemetry, analytics, cloud sync, or automatic background data collection.

Features:
• daily portion tracking
• overview and statistics for your own history
• portion guidance using hand and household measures
• local backup and restore through Android's document picker
• local PDF reports
• German, English, Spanish, French, and European Portuguese
• light, dark, and cozy pastel themes
• an optional local companion, disabled by default and backed up separately

The companion uses only locally earned in-app tokens. There are no purchases, ads, or paid unlocks.

Portion information is general orientation only. Esca Agnellis is not medical, dietary, or individualized nutritional advice and is not an official BZfE or BLE product.

The application code and build scripts are free software under GPL-3.0-only.
